This spreadsheet presents the UK Monetary Policy Event-Study Database (UKMPD) as outlined in Braun, Miranda-Agrippino and Saha. 
It captures high-frequency monetary policy surprises and monetary policy factors for the United Kingdom. Both the 'surprises' and 'factors' tab has an 'isMPC' identifier that takes the value TRUE for MPC announcements, and FALSE for press conferences. All datetimes are reported in GMT. 
The 'Summary of Asset Prices' tab provides a description of the various asset prices feeding into the dataset. 
If using the data, please cite as: Braun, Miranda-Agrippino and Saha, "Measuring Monetary Policy in the UK: the UK Monetary Policy Event-Study Database", Journal of Monetary Economics (2024)

Underlying data source: LSEG (formerly Refinitiv) Tick History and author calculations.

Notes:
10 July 2024: The database was revised for publication in the Journal of Monetary Economics. We now include two types of events: MPC announcements (which will capture Inflation Report (IR) / Monetary Policy Report (MPR) announcements in the post-2015 sample) and press conferences (which will capture IR/MPR announcements in the pre-2015 sample).
8 February 2024: We have revised the dataset to remove 3 observations in 2020, for the May, August and November MPC announcements and MPR publications which occured at 7am local time. These had previously been recorded as a 12pm local time publication. As 7am falls outside of UK financial market trading hours, data is not available at that time. There are also underlying data availability issues for MPC events on 9th July 1998, 8th March 2001, 10th April 2003 and 11th March 2020, which result in missing observations on those dates. The dataset is currently being revised for publication and may evolve.
